===== FOLLOW-UP: PICK TRIALS (5 attempts, added after the 8-task run) =====
Goal: pick the banana and the apple one at a time, one per arm, with a stronger grip.
All trials GUI-recorded. Success = each named fruit raised >5 cm above its start height.

1. "pick up the banana and the apple, one in each hand" 300 s -- FAIL
   apple dz -0.1 cm, banana dz -1.6 cm (banana slid 7.2 cm). No lift.
2. "pick up the banana with the left arm and the apple with the right arm" 180 s -- FAIL
   apple dz -0.1 cm, banana dz -2.3 cm.
3. "pick up the banana, then pick up the apple" 240 s, fingers 60 N -- FAIL
   apple dz +0.1 cm, banana dz -1.6 cm. Log showed "0 finger pads": pad boost did not apply yet.
4. Same task, 240 s, fingers 60 N, pads 4.0 (now really applied, 286 geoms) -- FAIL
   apple dz +0.0 cm, banana +3.5 cm (touched, not lifted).
   Grasp probe now correct: right gripper had 5159 closing-step contacts with the banana,
   grasp site within 2.9 cm of the banana centre; left site within 2.3 cm of the apple.
5. Same task, 180 s, fingers 80 N, pads 6.0, fruit surfaces 3.0 -- FAIL
   apple dz -0.1 cm, banana -1.6 cm. Contacts: left 87 on the apple, right 621 on the banana;
   closest site distances 2.1 cm (apple) and 2.9 cm (banana), both above the table.

What this shows honestly:
- The policy does bring both grippers down onto the correct objects (2-3 cm from centre) and the
  fingers do make sustained contact, so the objects are between the pads.
- It does not close/lift: every attempt ends with both fruits on the table, vertical change under
  2 cm in either direction. Raising finger force 22 -> 80 N, pad friction 1.6 -> 6.0 and fruit
  friction to 3.0 did not change that.
- Grip is therefore not limited by friction here; the arm is not producing enough upward motion
  after contact. That is a policy/action-range issue, not a physics-setting issue, and it cannot be
  fixed without scripting the motion (which would no longer be Dream-1's own behaviour).
- Budget: $4.870 of $5.00 spent after trial 5. Further paid trials were stopped at the cap.

NOTE ON THE PROBE BUG: trials 1-8 and pick trials 1-3 printed "contacts 0 / touched nothing".
That zero was a name-matching bug (searching for "finger" in gripper body names), not evidence
that no contact occurred. The corrected probe is used from trial 4 onward; contacts are real there.
